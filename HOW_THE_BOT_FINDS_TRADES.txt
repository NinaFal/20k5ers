==============================================================
  HOW THE BOT FINDS TRADES DURING A SCAN
==============================================================

The bot scans each symbol using daily candles. For every bar
it runs the same sequence: determine direction, score
confluence, calculate levels, decide whether to signal.

==============================================================
STEP 1 — DETERMINE DIRECTION
==============================================================

The bot looks at three timeframes and asks whether each one
is bullish, bearish, or mixed.

  Timeframes: Monthly, Weekly, Daily

  Per timeframe it compares short-term vs long-term price
  averages and checks recent momentum. The side with more
  evidence wins. If tied, the result is "mixed".

  Direction decision:
    If the majority of timeframes agree → use that direction.
    If split → daily timeframe acts as tiebreaker.

==============================================================
STEP 2 — SCORE CONFLUENCE (14 FLAGS)
==============================================================

The bot runs 14 independent checks. Each returns True or
False. The confluence score = number of True flags.

The higher the score, the stronger the setup.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 1 — HTF BIAS
--------------------------------------------------------------

Do the higher timeframes agree with the chosen direction?

True if the monthly or weekly timeframe supports the trade,
not just the daily. Rewards setups where multiple timeframes
are aligned.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 2 — LOCATION
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is price near a meaningful support or resistance zone?

The bot checks swing highs and lows on the daily chart,
where price sits within the recent trading range, and
whether price is near a historically significant level
(tested many times over many years).

Bullish: price near the bottom of the range or near support.
Bearish: price near the top of the range or near resistance.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 3 — FIBONACCI
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is price inside a Fibonacci retracement zone of the last
significant swing move?

The bot anchors the Fibonacci tool to the most recent
impulse leg using Blueprint rules (tied to break of
structure candles). It then checks whether current price
is inside the golden pocket retracement zone.

If price is inside the zone → True.
If price is outside → False.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 4 — STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------

Does price action structure on the daily chart support
the trade direction?

For bullish: looks for higher highs, higher lows, or a
break of structure to the upside.

For bearish: looks for lower highs, lower lows, or a
break of structure to the downside.

The strongest form is a confirmed break of structure.
A single higher/lower high or low is still enough to pass.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 5 — CONFIRMATION (H4 CANDLE)
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is there a triggering candle pattern on the lower timeframe?

The bot checks the most recent candle on the 4H chart
(or daily if 4H is unavailable) for signs of intent.

Bullish patterns that pass: engulfing candle, pin bar with
long lower wick, 4H break of structure up, or a strong
bullish candle with a large body relative to its range.

Bearish patterns: same logic reversed.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 6 — R/R RATIO
--------------------------------------------------------------

Can a valid trade be structured with a positive risk/reward?

True if a valid entry, stop loss, and take profit can be
calculated. In practice this only fails when candle data
is corrupt or too short to work with.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 7 — ATR REGIME
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is current market volatility high enough to trade?

The bot compares today's ATR(14) to its historical range
over the past 100 days and calculates a percentile rank.

If volatility is above a minimum threshold → True (TREND).
If volatility is too low → False (RANGE).

This flag also controls how strict the confluence threshold
is: higher volatility = stricter requirement to enter.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 8 — PATTERN (N or V SHAPE)
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is there a recognisable impulse-pullback price pattern?

Bullish N pattern: an impulse move up, followed by a
pullback that forms a higher low without breaking the
prior swing low, showing the trend is continuing.

Bearish V pattern: an impulse move down, followed by a
bounce that forms a lower high, showing downtrend continuation.

The impulse must be significant relative to recent volatility.
The pullback must be meaningful but not too deep.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 9 — Z-SCORE
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is price statistically cheap or expensive relative to its
recent average?

The bot calculates how many standard deviations price sits
from its mean over a recent lookback period.

Bullish: True if price is sufficiently below the mean
         (cheap, good for a long entry).
Bearish: True if price is sufficiently above the mean
         (expensive, good for a short entry).

Prevents buying into already extended up-moves or selling
into already extended down-moves.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 10 — MITIGATED S/R
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is price at a "flipped" support or resistance level?

A mitigated zone is a level that:
  1. Previously acted as resistance or support
  2. Was then broken by price
  3. Is now being retested from the other side

These are among the most reliable levels because they have
been tested, confirmed, broken, and are now being retested.

Bullish: old resistance that price broke above and is now
         retesting as support (resistance turned support).
Bearish: old support that price broke below and is now
         retesting as resistance (support turned resistance).

Price must be close to the level to pass.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 11 — STRUCTURAL FRAMEWORK (CHANNEL)
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is the market moving inside a clear ascending or descending
channel, and is price at the optimal side of that channel?

The bot connects recent swing highs and swing lows to detect
whether price is trending in a structured channel.

Bullish + ascending channel: True only if price is near
the lower boundary of the channel (buy the dip).

Bearish + descending channel: True only if price is near
the upper boundary of the channel (sell the rally).

Being in the middle of the channel does not pass.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 12 — DISPLACEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------

Was there a strong institutional impulse candle recently?

The bot looks at the last few candles for one whose body
(open to close distance) is large relative to recent
average volatility.

A big candle in the direction of the trade signals that
real order flow is behind the move, not just noise.

Bullish: a large green candle recently.
Bearish: a large red candle recently.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 13 — CANDLE REJECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is the most recent candle showing rejection of a price level?

The bot checks the shape of the last candle for signs that
price was pushed away from a level.

Bullish rejections: pin bar with long lower wick, bullish
engulfing, or hammer pattern.

Bearish rejections: pin bar with long upper wick, bearish
engulfing, or shooting star.

The wick must be meaningfully larger than the candle body
for a pin bar to qualify.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 14 — MOMENTUM
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is price momentum aligned with the trade direction?

The bot calculates rate of change over two windows:
a medium-term and a short-term lookback.

Bullish: passes if both windows show upward momentum
         (trend following), OR if medium-term is down but
         short-term is already turning up (early bounce).

Bearish: passes if both windows show downward momentum,
         OR if medium-term is up but short-term turning down.

==============================================================
STEP 3 — CALCULATE TRADE LEVELS
==============================================================

ENTRY:
  Calculated at the Fibonacci golden pocket of the last swing
  leg. If price is already near that level, the bot enters
  at market. Otherwise a limit order is placed.

STOP LOSS:
  Based on the nearest structural swing point (using H4 or
  daily candles), with a small buffer beyond it.
  Falls back to an ATR-based distance if no structure found.

TAKE PROFIT:
  Multiple TP levels scaled as multiples of the risk (R).
  Ranges from a quick partial target to an extended runner.

==============================================================
STEP 4 — SIGNAL DECISION
==============================================================

Confluence score and a quality factor are both checked.

If score AND quality both exceed the threshold → ACTIVE signal
  → bot places a limit order at the entry level.

If score is just below threshold → WATCHING
  → logged but no order placed.

Below watching threshold → ignored entirely.

Certain high-volatility instruments receive a small boost
to their score to account for their naturally wider ranges.

==============================================================
STEP 5 — ENTRY FILL
==============================================================

After an active signal, the bot waits several trading days
for price to reach the limit entry level.

If price reaches the level → order fills.
If price never reaches it  → trade is skipped. No chasing.

==============================================================
GENERAL CONDITIONS TO FIRE A TRADE
==============================================================

  - Sufficient candle history available
  - Multi-timeframe direction confirmed
  - Confluence score above regime threshold
  - Quality factor above minimum
  - Valid trade levels calculable
  - Within allowed trading hours
  - Daily loss limit not breached
  - Maximum concurrent trades not reached
  - No existing position in this symbol
  - Price fills the limit order within the wait window
