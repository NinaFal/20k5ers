==============================================================
  HOW THE BOT FINDS TRADES DURING A SCAN
  Source: strategy_core.py
==============================================================

The bot scans each symbol bar by bar through historical or live
daily candles. For every bar it runs the same sequence:
determine direction, score confluence, calculate levels, decide
whether to fire a signal. Minimum 50 daily candles required.

==============================================================
STEP 1 — DETERMINE DIRECTION
==============================================================

The bot looks at three timeframes independently and asks:
is this timeframe bullish, bearish, or mixed?

Timeframes used:
  - Monthly candles
  - Weekly candles
  - Daily candles (last 10 bars)

How trend is determined per timeframe (_infer_trend):

  1. Short SMA (last 8 closes) vs Long SMA (last 21 closes)
     - Short > Long  → bullish point
     - Short < Long  → bearish point

  2. Current close vs Long SMA
     - Price above long SMA → bullish point
     - Price below long SMA → bearish point

  3. Momentum check (last 10 bars)
     - Close breaks above the highest close of the prior 9 bars → bullish point
     - Close breaks below the lowest close of the prior 9 bars  → bearish point

Result: whichever side has more points wins. If tied → "mixed".

Direction decision (_pick_direction_from_bias):
  - 2 or 3 timeframes bullish  → trade direction = BULLISH
  - 2 or 3 timeframes bearish  → trade direction = BEARISH
  - Split / mixed              → use daily as tiebreaker
                                 if daily is also mixed → default bullish

This direction is then used for all checks in Step 2.

==============================================================
STEP 2 — SCORE CONFLUENCE (14 FLAGS)
==============================================================

The bot runs 14 checks. Each check returns True or False.
The confluence score = number of True flags.

Below is every check explained in full detail.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 1 — HTF BIAS
--------------------------------------------------------------

Already calculated in Step 1. Did at least 2 of the 3
timeframes agree on the chosen direction?

  True  → monthly, weekly, or daily (at least 2) are aligned
  False → only 1 timeframe agrees (e.g. daily alone)

This flag rewards trade direction being supported by the
higher timeframes, not just the daily.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 2 — LOCATION
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is price currently near a key support or resistance zone?

The bot checks several things:

  A. Swing pivots on the daily chart (last 50 candles)
     A swing high = candle whose high is the highest of the
     3 candles before AND after it.
     Same logic for swing lows.
     The bot keeps the last 5 swing highs and lows.

  B. Range position
     recent_high = highest high of last 20 bars
     recent_low  = lowest low  of last 20 bars
     range_size  = recent_high - recent_low

     Bullish: is price in the bottom 30% of the range?
              (price - recent_low) < range_size * 0.3
     Bearish: is price in the top 30% of the range?
              (recent_high - price) < range_size * 0.3

  C. Historical S/R from 20+ years of data (if available)
     Tolerance = 0.5% of current price
     Checks monthly S/R levels first (top 20)
     Then weekly S/R levels (top 30)
     Each level has a "touches" count. More touches = stronger.

  Result:
    Bullish True  → price is near a swing low, OR in the
                    bottom 30% of range, OR at a historical level
    Bearish True  → price is near a swing high, OR in the
                    top 30% of range, OR at a historical level

  Zone tolerance for swing pivots = 0.5 × ATR(14)

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 3 — FIBONACCI
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is price inside the Fibonacci retracement zone of the last
significant swing leg?

Step 1: Find the swing leg.

  The bot first tries to find a proper Blueprint anchor:

  For BULLISH (Bullish N pattern anchor):
    - Scan the last 20 candles for a Break of Structure UP
      (a green candle that closes above the highest high of
       the prior 5 candles)
    - Find the swing low just before that BOS candle
    - At that swing low, find a red candle followed by a
      green candle (the transition point)
    - Fib LOW  = close of the red candle (or swing low itself)
    - Fib HIGH = close of the BOS candle

  For BEARISH (Bearish V pattern anchor):
    - Same logic reversed: BOS down, swing high before it,
      green-to-red transition
    - Fib HIGH = open of the red candle at swing high
    - Fib LOW  = close of the BOS down candle

  Fallback (if no BOS found):
    Use the most recent swing high and low from pivots.
    If no pivots either: use max high / min low of last 30 bars.

Step 2: Check if price is in the zone.

  span = fib_high - fib_low

  Bullish retracement levels (price pulling back from high):
    0.382 = fib_high - span * 0.382
    0.500 = fib_high - span * 0.500
    0.618 = fib_high - span * 0.618  ← Golden Pocket top
    0.786 = fib_high - span * 0.786  ← Golden Pocket bottom

  True if price is between 0.786 and 0.382 retracement
  (the "golden pocket" zone for pullback entries).
  Also True if between 0.500 and 0.618 specifically.

  Same logic mirrored for bearish setups.

  If an external Fibonacci analysis module is available
  (analyze_fib_setup), that is used first and must confirm
  "in_golden_zone" = True.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 4 — STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------

Does the price action structure on the daily chart support
the trade direction?

The bot identifies swing highs and lows over the last 30
daily candles (pivot lookback = 3 bars each side).

For BULLISH — any of these passes:
  1. Break of Structure UP (BOS up):
     Last close > highest of the last 3 swing highs
     → "BOS up confirmed"

  2. Higher High AND Higher Low both present:
     swing_highs[-1] > swing_highs[-2]
     AND swing_lows[-1]  > swing_lows[-2]
     → "HH/HL pattern"

  3. Higher Low only:
     swing_lows[-1] > swing_lows[-2]
     → "Higher low formed"

For BEARISH — mirror image:
  1. BOS down: Last close < lowest of last 3 swing lows
  2. Lower High AND Lower Low
  3. Lower High only

Requires at least 2 swing highs and 2 swing lows in the
window. If fewer pivot points exist → False.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 5 — CONFIRMATION (H4 CANDLE)
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is there a triggering candle pattern on the 4H timeframe?

The bot uses H4 candles if available (needs at least 5 bars).
If not, it falls back to the last 10 daily candles.

It compares the last candle (curr) vs the one before (prev).

Measurements:
  body      = |close - open|
  full_range = high - low
  body_ratio = body / full_range

For BULLISH — any of these passes:
  1. Bullish engulfing:
     curr is green (close > open)
     prev is red   (close < open)
     curr.close > prev.open
     curr.open  < prev.close
     → Full body of curr wraps the full body of prev

  2. Bullish pin bar / hammer:
     lower_wick > body × 2
     lower_wick > upper_wick × 1.5
     → Long tail below showing rejection of lows

  3. Break of structure on H4:
     curr.high > highest high of the prior 4 H4 candles
     AND curr is a green candle

  4. Strong bullish candle:
     curr is green AND body_ratio > 0.6
     (body is more than 60% of the full candle range)

For BEARISH — mirror image:
  bearish engulfing, bearish pin bar, H4 BOS down,
  strong bearish candle with body_ratio > 0.6.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 6 — R/R RATIO (RISK/REWARD)
--------------------------------------------------------------

Can a valid trade be constructed with a positive R/R?

This flag is True if a valid entry, stop loss, and take
profit can be calculated (see Step 3 for full details).

If no valid swing leg exists AND no ATR-based SL is possible
(ATR <= 0), this flag is False.

In practice this flag fails only when candle data is corrupt
or there are fewer than ~20 usable bars.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 7 — ATR REGIME
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is market volatility high enough to trade?

Method:
  Calculate ATR(14) for each of the last 100 days.
  Sort those 100 ATR values.
  Find the percentile rank of today's ATR.

  Example: if today's ATR is higher than 65 of the 100
  historical ATRs → ATR percentile = 65.

  Default minimum percentile: 20
  (today's ATR must be in at least the 20th percentile)

Note: This flag also controls the confluence threshold.
  - If ATR percentile is OK (above minimum) → TREND regime
    → minimum confluence needed = 6 out of 14
  - If ATR percentile is too low → RANGE regime
    → minimum confluence needed = 4 out of 14

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 8 — PATTERN (N or V shape)
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is there a recognizable impulse-pullback price pattern?

For BULLISH — the "Bullish N" pattern:
  Window: last 15 candles (lookback=10 + 5)
  Pivot lookback: 2 bars each side

  Checks:
  1. Higher Low:
     Most recent swing low > previous swing low
  2. Significant Impulse:
     Range from prev_low to last swing high > 1.5 × ATR(14)
  3. Valid Pullback:
     Current price has retraced 20%–70% of the impulse range
     (not too shallow, not too deep)

  True if Higher Low AND Significant Impulse are both present.
  Pullback depth adds to the description but is not strictly
  required for the flag to be True.

For BEARISH — the "Bearish V" pattern:
  Same logic reversed:
  Lower High, significant impulse down, pullback 20–70%.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 9 — Z-SCORE
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is price statistically cheap (bullish) or expensive (bearish)
relative to its recent mean?

Calculation (last 20 daily closes):
  mean     = average of last 20 closes
  std_dev  = standard deviation of last 20 closes
  z-score  = (current_price - mean) / std_dev

Interpretation:
  Bullish trade:
    True if z-score < -1.0
    → Price is more than 1 standard deviation BELOW the mean
    → Price is relatively cheap, good for a long entry

  Bearish trade:
    True if z-score > +1.0
    → Price is more than 1 standard deviation ABOVE the mean
    → Price is relatively expensive, good for a short entry

This filter prevents buying into an already extended up-move
or selling into an already extended down-move.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 10 — MITIGATED S/R
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is price at a "flipped" support/resistance level?

A mitigated zone is a level that was:
  1. A prior swing high or low
  2. Then broken (price closed through it)
  3. Then retested (price returned to it)

These are the most reliable S/R zones because they have been:
  - Tested as resistance/support
  - Confirmed as significant (caused a reversal)
  - Broken (showing a regime shift)
  - Now being retested from the other side

Types:
  - Resistance Turned Support (RTS): old swing high, price
    broke above it, now comes back to test it as support
    → Valid for BULLISH trades

  - Support Turned Resistance (STR): old swing low, price
    broke below it, now bounces back to test it as resistance
    → Valid for BEARISH trades

Proximity requirement: price must be within 2% of the level.

The bot scans ALL candles available (minimum 50 required)
to build this library of mitigated levels.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 11 — STRUCTURAL FRAMEWORK (CHANNEL)
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is the market inside an ascending or descending price channel,
and is price at the optimal end of that channel?

Method:
  Find all swing highs and lows in the full candle window
  (pivot lookback = 3 bars each side).

  Look at the last 5 swing highs and last 5 swing lows.

  Ascending channel:
    All of last 5 swing lows are rising AND
    All of last 5 swing highs are rising

  Descending channel:
    All of last 5 swing highs are falling AND
    All of last 5 swing lows are falling

Position within channel:
  price_position = (price - last_swing_low) /
                   (last_swing_high - last_swing_low)

  Bullish + Ascending channel:
    True only if price_position < 0.40
    (price is in the bottom 40% of the channel)

  Bearish + Descending channel:
    True only if price_position > 0.60
    (price is in the top 60% of the channel)

Requires at least 30 candles and 3+ swing points.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 12 — DISPLACEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------

Was there a strong institutional impulse candle recently?

The bot scans the last 5 candles for any candle whose body
size is at least 1.5 × ATR(14).

  body = |close - open|

  Bullish: a green candle (close > open) with body ≥ 1.5 ATR
  Bearish: a red candle   (close < open) with body ≥ 1.5 ATR

The note includes how large the candle was in ATR multiples
(e.g. "Strong bullish candle (2.3x ATR)").

This confirms that a decisive move has recently occurred,
suggesting real order flow behind the directional move.

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 13 — CANDLE REJECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is the most recent candle showing rejection of a price level?

Uses daily candles (or H4 if available). Checks the last bar.

Measurements:
  body        = |close - open|
  upper_wick  = high - max(close, open)
  lower_wick  = min(close, open) - low

For BULLISH — any of these passes:
  1. Bullish pin bar:
     lower_wick > body × 2
     lower_wick > upper_wick × 1.5
     (Long lower tail, small body, small upper wick)

  2. Bullish engulfing:
     curr is green, prev is red
     curr.close > prev.high (entire range)
     curr.open  < prev.low  (entire range)

  3. Hammer:
     curr is green AND lower_wick > body
     (Green candle with meaningful lower tail)

For BEARISH — mirror image:
  bearish pin bar (long upper wick),
  bearish engulfing (wraps entire prev candle),
  shooting star (red with upper wick > body).

--------------------------------------------------------------
FLAG 14 — MOMENTUM
--------------------------------------------------------------

Is price momentum aligned with the trade direction?

Two Rate of Change (ROC) values are calculated:

  ROC (10-bar): ((close_now - close_10_bars_ago) /
                  close_10_bars_ago) × 100

  ROC (5-bar):  ((close_now - close_5_bars_ago) /
                  close_5_bars_ago) × 100

For BULLISH — True in either of these cases:
  Case A (trend following):
    ROC(10) > 0 AND ROC(5) > 0
    → Both short and medium momentum are rising

  Case B (mean reversion):
    ROC(10) < -2% AND ROC(5) > 0
    → Medium term is down but short term is already
      turning up → early bounce signal

For BEARISH — mirror image:
  Case A: ROC(10) < 0 AND ROC(5) < 0
  Case B: ROC(10) > +2% AND ROC(5) < 0
           (medium up, short turning down → early reversal)

==============================================================
STEP 3 — CALCULATE TRADE LEVELS
==============================================================

ENTRY PRICE:

  Primary method (Fibonacci):
    Use the same swing leg found for Flag 3.
    Apply the 0.618 retracement level as the limit entry.

    Bullish entry = fib_high - (span × 0.618)
    Bearish entry = fib_low  + (span × 0.618)

    If current price is already within 0.3 ATR of this level,
    enter at market (current price) instead of placing a limit.

  Fallback (no swing leg found):
    Entry = current close price (market order).

STOP LOSS:

  Primary method (structure-based):
    Scan H4 candles (last 20 bars) for swing pivots.
    Falls back to daily if H4 not available.

    Bullish SL = lowest swing low of the last 20 H4 bars
                 minus 0.4 ATR buffer

    Bearish SL = highest swing high of the last 20 H4 bars
                 plus 0.4 ATR buffer

  Secondary method (ATR-based, used when no structure found):
    Bullish SL = entry - (ATR × atr_sl_multiplier)
    Bearish SL = entry + (ATR × atr_sl_multiplier)

  When both methods produce a result, the tighter (closer
  to entry) one is selected.

TAKE PROFIT LEVELS (R = entry - SL for bullish):

  TP1 = entry + 0.6R   (partial profit, early exit)
  TP2 = entry + 1.1R   (1:1 area, main partial)
  TP3 = entry + 1.8R   (full target)
  TP4 = entry + 2.8R   (extended target, TP3 + 1R)
  TP5 = entry + 3.8R   (maximum target, TP3 + 2R)

  (All mirrored for bearish: subtract instead of add)

==============================================================
STEP 4 — DECIDE SIGNAL STATUS
==============================================================

After scoring:

  confluence_score = number of True flags (max 14)
  quality_factors  = max(1, confluence_score // 3)

Volatile asset boost (XAUUSD, NAS100USD, GBPJPY, BTCUSD,
ETHUSD):
  If volatile_asset_boost parameter > 1.0:
    boosted_score   = int(score   × boost_multiplier)
    boosted_quality = int(quality × boost_multiplier)
  This lets high-volatility assets pass thresholds more easily.

Thresholds (from ATR regime flag):
  TREND regime  → need confluence >= 6  AND quality >= 3
  RANGE regime  → need confluence >= 4  AND quality >= 3

Signal outcomes:
  "active"   → passes both thresholds → bot places an order
  "watching" → confluence is 1 below threshold → logged only
  no signal  → below watching threshold → ignored

==============================================================
STEP 5 — ENTRY FILL LOGIC (LIMIT ORDERS)
==============================================================

Once an active signal fires, the bot places a limit order.

The bot waits up to 5 trading days for price to reach the
calculated entry level.

  Bullish limit: fills when price LOW <= entry level
  Bearish limit: fills when price HIGH >= entry level

If price never touches the entry level within 5 bars,
the trade is skipped entirely. The level is never adjusted
or chased.

==============================================================
FULL CHECKLIST — ALL CONDITIONS TO FIRE A TRADE
==============================================================

  1.  At least 50 daily candles available
  2.  Multi-timeframe direction can be determined
  3.  Confluence score passes regime threshold (4 or 6)
  4.  Quality factor >= 3
  5.  Valid entry, SL, and TP levels calculated
  6.  Session filter: 08:00–22:00 UTC only
  7.  Daily drawdown limit not exceeded
  8.  Maximum 7 concurrent open trades not reached
  9.  No existing open position in this symbol
  10. Price reaches the limit entry level within 5 days
