==============================================================
  HOW THE BOT FINDS TRADES DURING A SCAN
  Based on: strategy_core.py → generate_signals()
==============================================================

Every scan runs through this exact sequence for each symbol.
Minimum 50 daily candles required before any signal can fire.

--------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — DETERMINE DIRECTION (Multi-Timeframe Bias)
--------------------------------------------------------------

The bot checks trend on 3 timeframes independently:
  - Monthly candles
  - Weekly candles
  - Daily candles

For each timeframe, trend is determined by:
  1. Is the short SMA (8 bars) above the long SMA (21 bars)?
  2. Is current price above the long SMA?
  3. Is price making new highs (or lows) vs the last 10 bars?

Result per timeframe: "bullish", "bearish", or "mixed"

Direction rule:
  - 2 or more timeframes bullish  → trade bullish
  - 2 or more timeframes bearish  → trade bearish
  - Mixed / split                 → use daily direction as tiebreaker

--------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — SCORE CONFLUENCE (0–7 flags)
--------------------------------------------------------------

The bot scores up to 7 confluence factors.
Each one is either True (pass) or False (fail).
Score = number of True flags.

The 7 flags:

  1. HTF BIAS
     Are at least 2 of 3 timeframes aligned with trade direction?

  2. LOCATION
     Is price near a key support/resistance zone?
     Checks monthly highs/lows, weekly highs/lows, daily pivots,
     and historical S/R levels from 20+ years of data.
     Proximity threshold: within ~1–2% of the level.

  3. FIBONACCI
     Is price inside a Fibonacci retracement zone (0.382–0.886)
     of the most recent swing leg on weekly or daily chart?

  4. LIQUIDITY
     Always True (filter was removed from live bot).

  5. STRUCTURE
     Does market structure support the direction?
     Looks for breakouts, order blocks, or structure shifts
     on monthly/weekly/daily timeframes.

  6. CONFIRMATION
     Is there a confirming H4 candle at the entry zone?
     Checks for bullish/bearish engulfing or rejection candles.
     Falls back to daily if H4 data is unavailable.

  7. R/R RATIO
     Does the trade have a valid entry, SL, and TP?
     This is calculated as part of the confluence check
     (see Step 3 below). If no valid levels can be found → False.

--------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — CALCULATE TRADE LEVELS
--------------------------------------------------------------

Entry, SL, and TP are calculated during confluence scoring.

ENTRY:
  1. Find the last swing impulse leg on the daily chart
     (significant move up or down)
  2. Calculate 0.618 Fibonacci retracement of that leg
     - Bullish: entry = swing_high - (range × 0.618)
     - Bearish: entry = swing_low  + (range × 0.618)
  3. If current price is already within 0.3 ATR of the Fib level
     → use current price as entry (market order)
     Otherwise → use Fib level (limit order)

  Fallback (no swing leg found):
    Entry = current close price

STOP LOSS:
  Method 1 — Structure-based (preferred):
    Scan H4 candles (or daily, 20-bar lookback) for
    the nearest swing low (bull) or swing high (bear).
    SL = structure level ± 0.4 ATR buffer

  Method 2 — ATR-based (fallback):
    SL = entry ± (ATR × 1.5)

  When both are available, the tighter one wins.

TAKE PROFIT levels (R = risk = entry − SL):
  TP1 = entry + 0.6R
  TP2 = entry + 1.1R
  TP3 = entry + 1.8R
  TP4 = entry + (TP3_multiple + 1.0R)
  TP5 = entry + (TP3_multiple + 2.0R)

--------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 — DECIDE SIGNAL STATUS
--------------------------------------------------------------

After scoring:

  "active" signal:
    boosted_confluence >= min_confluence threshold
    AND quality_factors >= min_quality_factors

  "watching" signal:
    boosted_confluence >= min_confluence - 1
    (close to threshold but not quite)

  No signal:
    Below watching threshold → ignored

Confluence thresholds (regime-dependent):
  Trend regime (ATR percentile OK)  → need 6 out of 7
  Range regime (ATR percentile low) → need 4 out of 7

Quality factor:
  quality = max(1, confluence_score // 3)
  Must be >= min_quality_factors (default: 3)

Volatile asset boost:
  For Gold, Nasdaq, GBP/JPY, BTC, ETH:
  confluence score gets a +1 boost before threshold check
  to allow more entries on high-volatility instruments.

--------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 — WAIT FOR ENTRY FILL (Live Bot)
--------------------------------------------------------------

Once an "active" signal is generated:

  The bot places a LIMIT ORDER at the calculated entry level.
  It then waits up to 5 bars (5 trading days) for price to fill.

  Bullish: fills when price drops down TO the entry level
  Bearish: fills when price rises UP TO the entry level

  If price never reaches the level in 5 bars → trade is skipped.
  The level is never adjusted or chased.

--------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY — WHAT MAKES A TRADE FIRE
--------------------------------------------------------------

  1. At least 50 daily candles available
  2. 2 of 3 timeframes agree on direction
  3. Confluence score passes threshold for current regime
  4. Quality factor passes (confluence // 3 >= 3)
  5. Valid entry, SL, and TP levels can be calculated
  6. Session filter: scan runs 08:00–22:00 UTC only
  7. Daily drawdown limit not exceeded
  8. Max 7 concurrent open trades not reached
  9. No existing position in that symbol
  10. Price reaches limit entry level within 5 trading days
