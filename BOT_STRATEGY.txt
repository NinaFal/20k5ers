==============================================================
  LIVE BOT STRATEGY OVERVIEW
  20k5ers — 5ers 60K High Stakes Challenge Bot
==============================================================

This document describes the core strategy used by the main live bot.
Use it as a starting point to build your own bot on top of this logic.

--------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT KIND OF STRATEGY IS THIS?
--------------------------------------------------------------

Confluence-based, regime-adaptive trend/range trading system.
- Trades during London + New York sessions only
- Uses ADX to detect market regime (trending vs ranging)
- Enters at Fibonacci golden pocket retracements (0.618)
- Exits in 5 partial layers to lock in profit progressively
- Risk is dynamically reduced as daily drawdown increases

--------------------------------------------------------------
2. MARKET REGIME DETECTION (ADX-Based)
--------------------------------------------------------------

The bot first determines which "mode" the market is in:

  TREND MODE    → ADX >= 25
                  High directional movement
                  Requires confluence score of 6/7 to enter

  RANGE MODE    → ADX < 20
                  Ranging/consolidating market
                  Ultra-conservative; requires confluence 3+/7
                  Also needs: price at S/R, Fib 0.786 zone,
                  H4 rejection candle, low ATR environment

  TRANSITION    → ADX between 20-25
                  Uncertain; requires high confluence (6+/7)
                  Optional: early entry if ADX slope rising
                  and a recent +DI/-DI crossover detected

--------------------------------------------------------------
3. ENTRY — FIBONACCI GOLDEN POCKET
--------------------------------------------------------------

Once regime is identified and confluence passes:

  1. Detect last swing impulse (significant high/low structure)
  2. Calculate Fibonacci retracement of that swing
  3. Place a LIMIT ORDER at the 0.618 retracement level
     (Optional: fine-tune price with an ATR-based offset)
  4. If price doesn't reach the entry level within 5 bars → skip trade

Session filter: Only trade 08:00–22:00 UTC (London open to NY close)
No trades during Asian session or after Friday 16:00 UTC

--------------------------------------------------------------
4. STOP LOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------

Two methods, uses whichever is tighter/more structured:

  Method 1 (preferred) — Structure-based:
    Scan H4 or daily candles (20-bar lookback)
    Place SL just below swing low (bull) or above swing high (bear)
    Offset: 0.4 × ATR beyond the structure level

  Method 2 (fallback) — ATR-based:
    SL = Entry ± (ATR × 1.5)

Risk per trade = abs(entry price − stop loss)

--------------------------------------------------------------
5. TAKE PROFIT — 5-LAYER PARTIAL EXIT SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------

Take profit is split into 5 levels based on R-multiples of risk:

  TP1 → 0.6R profit  → close 20% of position
  TP2 → 1.1R profit  → close 60% of position
  TP3 → 1.8R profit  → close 10% of position
  TP4 → 2.8R profit  → close  5% of position
  TP5 → 3.8R profit  → close  5% of position

Stop loss moves up progressively as TPs are hit:

  After TP1 hit → SL moves to breakeven (+0.05R)
  After TP2 hit → SL moves to TP1 level (0.6R)
  After TP3 hit → SL moves to TP1 level (0.6R)
  After TP4 hit → SL moves to TP2 level (1.1R)
  After TP5 hit → SL moves to TP3 level (1.8R)

Optional ATR trailing stop activates at 2.2R profit,
trailing at 1.5 × ATR distance.

--------------------------------------------------------------
6. CONFLUENCE SCORING (0–7)
--------------------------------------------------------------

Before entering, the bot scores 7 factors.
Minimum score required depends on regime (see section 2).

The 7 factors are:
  1. Location context      — price near key S/R zone
  2. Fibonacci context     — price in 0.618–0.886 retracement zone
  3. Liquidity             — liquidity pool nearby
  4. Structure alignment   — price action structure confirms direction
  5. Confirmation candle   — H4 candle confirms direction at entry
  6. ATR regime            — volatility is in a tradeable range
  7. Risk/reward ratio     — R:R is acceptable

Each factor = 1 point. Score is simply the count of passing factors.

--------------------------------------------------------------
7. POSITION SIZING
--------------------------------------------------------------

  risk_usd        = account_balance × 0.6%
  stop_pips       = abs(entry − stop_loss) / pip_size
  risk_per_lot    = stop_pips × pip_value_per_lot
  lot_size        = risk_usd / risk_per_lot

If multiple positions are open simultaneously,
lot size is reduced proportionally.

--------------------------------------------------------------
8. RISK MANAGEMENT — GRADUATED DRAWDOWN SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------

The bot watches daily drawdown in real time and acts in tiers:

  Tier 1 (2% daily DD)   → Reduce risk per trade to 0.4% (67%)
  Tier 2 (3.5% daily DD) → Cancel all pending limit orders
  Tier 3 (4.5% daily DD) → Emergency close worst open positions

Additional rules:
  - Hard stop on new trades if daily loss limit approached (4.1%)
  - If account profit reaches 9% → reduce risk to 0.25% (protect gains)
  - Max 7 concurrent open trades
  - No new trades Friday 16:00+ UTC
  - Close all positions Friday 22:00+ UTC if daily DD >= 2%

--------------------------------------------------------------
9. INSTRUMENTS TRADED
--------------------------------------------------------------

  Forex (35+):   All major pairs and crosses
                 EUR, GBP, USD, JPY, CHF, AUD, NZD, CAD

  Metals:        XAU_USD (Gold), XAG_USD (Silver)

  Indices:       NAS100_USD (Nasdaq), UK100_USD (FTSE)

  Crypto:        BTC_USD, ETH_USD, XRP_USD, ADA_USD

Note: Volatile assets (Gold, Crypto, Indices) get a 1.5× boost
to their confluence threshold to allow more entries.

--------------------------------------------------------------
10. KEY PARAMETERS TO TUNE
--------------------------------------------------------------

  adx_trend_threshold    = 25.0      # ADX level for trend mode
  adx_range_threshold    = 20.0      # ADX level for range mode
  entry_fib_level        = 0.618     # Fibonacci entry retracement
  atr_sl_multiplier      = 1.5       # ATR multiplier for fallback SL
  risk_per_trade_pct     = 0.6       # Base risk per trade (%)
  tp1_r_multiple         = 0.6       # First TP at 0.6R
  tp2_r_multiple         = 1.1       # Second TP at 1.1R
  tp3_r_multiple         = 1.8       # Third TP at 1.8R
  atr_trail_multiplier   = 1.5       # Trailing stop distance
  trail_activation_r     = 2.2       # Activate trail at 2.2R profit
  tier1_dd_pct           = 2.0       # First drawdown warning
  tier2_dd_pct           = 3.5       # Cancel pending orders
  tier3_dd_pct           = 4.5       # Emergency close positions

--------------------------------------------------------------
11. STRATEGY FLOW SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Scan market → Calculate ADX → Determine regime
  2. Score confluence (0–7) → Check minimum threshold for regime
  3. If score passes → Calculate Fib entry, SL, TP levels
  4. Check session filter + daily DD + max open trades
  5. Place pending limit order at 0.618 Fib level
  6. Monitor fill (5-bar window)
  7. Once filled → Manage 5 TP levels + trailing SL
  8. Monitor drawdown tiers in real time → Adjust or close if needed
